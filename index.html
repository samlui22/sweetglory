Policies

Allergens

All cakes, fillings and icings may contain, or come into contact with soy, wheat, dairy, nuts, or other allergens.

It is the customer’s responsibility to inform Sweet Glory prior to the confirmation of their booking of any allergy issues. It is also the responsibility of the customer to inform their guests of all allergy information. We will not be held liable for any allergic reaction resulting from consumption of the cake.

Cancellation Policy

We require 48 hours notice from pick-up date to cancel an order.

All refunds are given as two options:

1) Full refund for store credit
2) $4 cancellation fee for refund to original payment method
